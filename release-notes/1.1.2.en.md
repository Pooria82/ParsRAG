### New

- Once a day the app checks whether a newer version is out. If one is, it shows what changed and moves to the latest version with one command, after taking a backup. You can turn the check off in Settings.
- After every update, this window shows what changed.
- PowerPoint speaker notes, CSV and TSV files as tables, and previews of uploaded images are now read and shown.

### Fixed

- Answers are written in the language of the question, even when the documents are in the other language.
- Your messages sit on the side their language starts from: Persian on the right, English on the left.
- When Ollama is not running, the model is not installed, or the API key is rejected, the sidebar says so instead of "Service ready".
- Text that already arrived is kept when the connection drops or times out, and long answers are no longer cut off while the model is still working.
- Deleting a conversation works while the service is offline and finishes on the server later. Two open tabs no longer overwrite each other.
- Keyboard shortcuts work with the Persian keyboard layout.
- Unsaved server-setting edits are no longer lost when the page refreshes.
